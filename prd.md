# Classic Biryani Kabab & Curry Website

## Product Requirements Document

---

# 1. Project Overview

Classic Biryani Kabab & Curry is a premium Indian and Hyderabadi restaurant located in Manchester, New Hampshire, offering:

* Dine-In
* Takeout
* Delivery
* Catering Services

The website should position the restaurant as the destination for authentic Hyderabadi cuisine while generating catering leads and increasing online orders.

Theme:

Royal Taste of Hyderabad

Style:

Luxury black and gold with subtle Hyderabadi and Nizami architectural influences.

---

## Primary Goal
